FraudGraph Final Review
Final Year Project · B.Tech CSE (AI & ML)

Fraudulent User & Fake Review Detection in E-commerce using Graph Neural Networks

We model reviewers, products and reviews as a multi-relational graph and catch coordinated fraud that row-by-row models miss.

SwastayanRitamHimanshuRishiraj
Problem

Fake reviews come in rings, not alone

#2

Online shopping was the second most reported fraud category with the US FTC in 2025.

$48B

Estimated global e-commerce fraud losses in 2025 (Juniper Research).

~9.5%

Share of fraudulent users in the Amazon benchmark: a heavily imbalanced problem.

Fraudsters share products, rating patterns and review text. These links are invisible to a classifier that looks at one user at a time, but they are edges in a graph.

Objectives

What we set out to do

Model the data as a graph

Build multi-relational user and review graphs from Amazon and YelpChi.

Train GNN detectors

Implement GCN, GraphSAGE and CARE-GNN for node classification.

Beat feature-only baselines

Compare against Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost.

Ship a working demo

A Streamlit app that scores a user and shows their neighbourhood.

Datasets

Two public, labelled benchmarks

System Architecture

From raw reviews to a fraud score

STAGE 1Load dataset and relations
STAGE 2Normalise features, 40/20/40 stratified split
STAGE 3Build homogeneous and multi-relation graphs
STAGE 4GNN message passing (2 layers, 64 hidden)
STAGE 5Classifier with class-weighted loss
STAGE 6Threshold tuning, evaluation, demo

Stack

Python 3.11, PyTorch 2.3, DGL 2.1, scikit-learn, XGBoost, Streamlit.

Compute

Google Colab, T4 GPU. Full CARE-GNN run on Amazon took about 6 minutes.

Training

Adam, lr 0.01, weight decay 1e-3, early stopping on validation AUC.

Models

Baselines vs. graph models

Baseline

LR · RF · XGBoost

Use node features only. No knowledge of who is connected to whom.

GNN

GCN · GraphSAGE

Aggregate neighbour features over a merged graph. GraphSAGE samples neighbours and keeps the node's own features separate.

Fraud-aware

CARE-GNN

Filters out dissimilar (camouflaged) neighbours per relation using a learned similarity, then aggregates relations.

Conclusion & Future Scope

Graph structure helps, if the model handles camouflage

Findings

  • CARE-GNN improved AUC by 2.8 points on Amazon and 5.2 on YelpChi over XGBoost.
  • Plain GCN underperformed, because fraudsters connect to genuine users to blend in.
  • Weighted loss with threshold tuning raised fraud recall by about 11 points.

Next steps

  • Try newer models such as PC-GNN and BWGNN.
  • Add review-text embeddings (e.g. Sentence-BERT) as node features.
  • Explain predictions with GNNExplainer.
